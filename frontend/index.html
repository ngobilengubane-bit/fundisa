Why Fundisa exists

Talent shouldn't lose to a closing date.

Students should not have to choose between staying in class and paying for registration, accommodation, food, transport, textbooks, data, or the next semester. Yet many are left searching the internet for funding and finding pages that are outdated, already closed, or simply irrelevant to their course and circumstances. By the time a student finds the right opportunity, the deadline can already be gone.

Fundisa exists to change that experience. We bring relevant funding opportunities into one place, make deadlines visible, help students understand what an opportunity is really asking for, and give them tools to prepare and keep moving when one route closes. The goal is simple: help students avoid missing the funding that could keep their education moving.

Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

Is Fundisa affiliated with NSFAS, Sasol, or the other bursary providers listed?

No • Fundisa is an independent, student-built directory. We link to real, currently-open bursaries so you can find and apply to them. Always confirm deadlines and requirements on the provider's own site before applying, since those can change on their end.

How current is the bursary information?

Fundisa combines automated bursary data feeds with freshness checks against public provider information. Listings can still change at the source, so use the "Learn more" link on any bursary to confirm the latest deadline and requirements before you apply.

How does the AI bursary matching work, and is my data used for anything?

You describe your field of study, and Fundisa sends that description • along with its bursary list • to Google's Gemini AI to suggest the closest fits. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use submitted prompts to improve their products, so avoid including anything you wouldn't want reviewed. It isn't stored anywhere by Fundisa beyond that one request.

Will the AI letter assistant write my whole application for me?

No. It drafts a first version based only on what you tell it about yourself, and is explicitly built to never invent achievements, grades, or circumstances you didn't mention. Read it over, personalize it, and check every detail before you submit it anywhere • it's a starting point, not a finished product.

Does Fundisa process donations for the student fundraisers?

No. Every fundraiser links out to the student's own real donation page • BackaBuddy, GoFundMe, a bank account, whatever they already use. Fundisa lists the story; the money never passes through Fundisa itself.

Is Fundisa free to use?

Yes, entirely. Browsing bursaries, the AI assistant, the letter helper, and the fundraiser listings cost nothing to use.

I'm a student • how do I get my bursary or fundraiser listed?

Right now listings go through the Fundisa team directly rather than a public submission form • reach out with the details and it'll be reviewed and added.

I found an error in a listing • what should I do?

Please flag it. Accuracy matters most here, since this is information students use to make real decisions • reach out to the Fundisa team with what looks wrong and it'll be corrected.
