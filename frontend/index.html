Why Fundisa exists

Talent shouldn't lose to a closing date.

Every year, capable students miss out on funding they'd have qualified for — not because they weren't good enough, but because they never found the right bursary in time, didn't know how to put their story into a motivational letter, or hit a cost too specific for any bursary to cover. Fundisa exists to close that gap: real, current bursaries in one place, AI help with the parts that intimidate people most, and a way for community to step in when the system moves too slowly to help.

Meet Our Founder & CEO

Nqobile Ngubani

Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Fundisa

Driven by a passion for technology, systems design, and educational empowerment, Nqobile Ngubani founded Fundisa to solve one of the most critical challenges facing students today: accessible financial aid and educational resources.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, Nqobile combines technical proficiency in software architecture and database design with a deep personal commitment to community upliftment. Having witnessed firsthand the financial hurdles that talented students encounter—from registration deposits to textbook costs—Nqobile engineered Fundisa to bridge the gap between ambitious learners, funding opportunities, and community support.

"Education should be determined by talent and determination, not financial constraint. Fundisa was built to ensure every student has the tools, funding, and community backing needed to reach graduation." — Nqobile Ngubani
